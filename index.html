Levels

    How this page works

    The chart shows NQ futures candles. It refreshes by itself every minute, but the candles are about 10 minutes behind the market (free futures data is always delayed). Use it to see where price sits against the levels, not to time an entry.

    The GEX levels come from the QQQ, SPY and NDX options and are converted to NQ prices. They are recalculated every 10 minutes and show up here within a few minutes. Before the 09:30 New York open the gamma flip is the last session's; today's comes after the open.

    Colours: blue = positive gamma (call side), gold = negative gamma (put side), magenta = gamma flip, grey = a level SPY and QQQ share.

    On your own TradingView chart instead: add the GEX Levels indicator (or GEX Levels Mobile on the phone). You get the same levels on your real-time chart, but they update only 4 times a day (new levels about 09:20, 10:55, 14:45 and 17:35 New York), and you have to remove and re-add the indicator each time. This page is the fastest way to see the newest levels.
